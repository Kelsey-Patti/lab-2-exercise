Answer written questions 1 and 2 here.

1. SFML's coordinate system has (0, 0) in the top left corner of the screen with the greatest 
   values for x and y in the bottom right corner. It is surprising that to move an object down on 
   the screen you have to increase the y value. This affects the angle calculations because it 
   reverses the direction the angle rotates (down instead of up).

2. By not modifying GameState inside ProcessInputs, each component has its own responsibility. 
   This makes code cleaner, more reusible, and more easily modifiable.
